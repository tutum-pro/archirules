# Verification register

What has been **verified**, as opposed to merely **asserted**. Started at the first defect
caused by an unverified claim — which is when it turns out to be needed.

Organised **by subject, not by category**: verification is about a thing (a library, a layer,
a component), not about a bucket. Collecting all "verified" claims into one table forces the
reader to hop between rows to understand a single subject.
